# Stripe Sense

Dial-in, throttle-stop and delay-box predictions for bracket and index racers - from your own runs and your own weather station.

## Download (Windows)

**[Download Stripe Sense for Windows (latest)](https://github.com/nhildahl/stripe-sense-releases/releases/latest/download/StripeSenseSetup.exe)** - or pick a version on the [Releases page](https://github.com/nhildahl/stripe-sense-releases/releases).

1. Run `StripeSenseSetup.exe`. It installs just for you - no administrator password needed.
2. Open Stripe Sense from the Start menu (or the desktop icon). A short setup asks for your car, your track and your weather station.
3. 30 days free with everything. After that, enter your license key in **Data > License**.

Stripe Sense checks here for updates and offers them by itself - your data is kept.

## What it does

- Predicts your **dial-in** (bracket) or **throttle-stop time** (index), and your **delay-box** setting, with a ± range and your breakout odds
- Learns your car from your runs: weather, 60 ft, stop time and wind effects, and how quick the car is right now
- Weather from your own station: Davis WeatherLink, Kestrel, Computech RaceAir, Altronics PerformAIRE, Ambient, Tempest, Ecowitt, any CSV log - or the National Weather Service
- 1/4 and 1/8 mile, class presets from Super Comp to Junior Dragster, about 140 US dragstrips built in
- Finish Line: who took the stripe and by how many inches, with a 3D driver's view
- Lanes screen with huge numbers, phone alerts, time slips
